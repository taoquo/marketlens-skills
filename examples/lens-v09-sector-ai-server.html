The AI server chain is still in structural expansion: NVIDIA data center revenue rose 110% year on year, TSMC's Q3 revenue rose 41% in US dollars at a 66.4% gross margin, and ASML posted a record €11.7B quarter. The profit pool is not evenly distributed, and the market is de-risking. The same conditions produce different rankings by multiple, implied assumption, and ROIIC. The overlap is still the bottleneck — foundry and advanced packaging. What changed since May is that the GPU has de-rated from 55x to 17x, so it no longer fails the valuation test; it fails the cash-conversion test.

Red Flags

  • NVIDIA's Q4 gross margin guide is 71-72%, down from 74.8% in Q3. The 14-week quarter flatters sequential revenue growth; the margin line is the first crack.
  • TSMC is spending $60-64B in 2026 while 2nm ramps and overseas fabs dilute margin. Capacity build is not value creation until ROIIC clears WACC.
  • NVIDIA deployed $58.1B into equity securities in the first nine months and agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9B. The ecosystem financing cuts both ways if AI demand slows.
  • ASML's record quarter still carries China and export-control exposure, and high-NA adoption is early. Do not annualize the record.

Decision Impact

  • The best expression is not 'all AI stocks'. Rank by implied assumption first, then overlay allocation and cash conversion.
  • GPU is no longer priced for perfection, but CFO/NI of 0.74 and receivables up 143% cap it at hold/watch. A lower multiple does not fix the earnings base.
  • Foundry is the cleanest bottleneck: 66.4% gross margin, 44% full-year revenue growth guidance, and visible capex. It remains the core expression.
  • Networking and custom silicon stay demanding on multiple and weak on allocation. Do not add them to the same cluster.
