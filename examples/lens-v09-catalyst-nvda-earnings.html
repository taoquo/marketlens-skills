The Q3 event is over, and it cuts both ways. Revenue of $115.8B beat the $108B guide and the $109.3B consensus; EPS of $2.70 beat $2.47; the Q4 guide of $130B beat the $127.7B consensus. The stock still closed down 2.9% the next day, fell to $124.50 by Nov 20, then jumped 31% on Nov 23 when the board reinstated the CEO. Operationally the thesis strengthened; the price was driven by margin compression and governance, not by the revenue gap. The next event is Q4 FY27 on Feb 23, 2027, with a ±22.2% implied move.

Red Flags

  • Red team: AI capex is increasingly debt-financed, and the 14-week Q4 flatters sequential growth. If Q4 gross margin falls below 71%, the revenue beat is a peak-margin print, not a positive gap.
  • Q3 operating cash flow covered only 0.74x net income, and receivables rose 143% year on year. The revenue gap is not yet a cash gap.
  • The CEO resigned on Nov 12 and was reinstated on Nov 23 after a board investigation. A new disclosure is a binary risk that no revenue model captures.
  • The 31% rally on the CEO return is a governance event, not an earnings signal. Do not chase it as thesis confirmation.
  • A ±22.2% implied move means a directional position needs a hedge or a smaller size. This is event risk, not edge.

Decision Impact

  • Q3 operational read: strengthened. Revenue, EPS, and the Q4 guide all cleared the bar, and data center grew 110% year on year.
  • Post-event label: event watch, not an upgrade. The margin guide and the governance record are unresolved, and the next event carries a ±22.2% implied move.
  • The next event is Q4 on Feb 23. The key data are gross margin, CFO/NI, and the Q1 FY28 guide; a revenue beat alone is already in the price.
  • The event score is not a trade instruction. The setup still needs a bounded downside and a dated review window.
