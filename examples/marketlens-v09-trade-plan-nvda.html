Red Flags

  • The upside case restates the implied assumption. This is the direct reason for the downgrade.
  • The probability lacks a reference class: no historical sample is given for a $200B-revenue company sustaining 20%+ growth for a decade.
  • The regime is tight or crowded, and the setup depends on beta and crowded momentum.
  • Plan return depends on management continuing to invest at high returns while the allocation read is only adequate. Liquidity is Deep, so it is not a cap.

Decision Impact

  • Research quality and Deep liquidity cannot pull the label back to tradable. Two overrides hold it at monitor closely.
  • The delivery-dependence discount cuts position intent one notch. It does not change the label.
  • The only upgrade paths are a price reset that makes the implied assumption acceptable, or new evidence the price does not contain.
  • A louder narrative without stronger evidence is not an upgrade. If the user holds a real position with high AI exposure, this becomes a portfolio-concentration question.
