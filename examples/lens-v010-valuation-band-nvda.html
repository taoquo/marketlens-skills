The band answers a narrow question: where does 16.7x sit in NVIDIA's own history? At the 0.5th percentile it is below the lower band of its trailing 750-observation window; almost nothing in the window is cheaper. The useful part is the decomposition: price rose 213% over the window, EPS rose 1,125%, and the multiple fell 74.4%. The move was entirely EPS-led. That is not a re-rating, and it is not proof of value. The band's denominator is peak-cycle earnings: TTM EPS of $9.31 is up 143% year on year, and Q3 operating cash flow covered only 0.74x net income. The band can say the multiple is low; it cannot say the earnings base is normal. The reverse DCF says only 6.2% growth is now required, which is undemanding; the earnings-quality screens are what keep the label at hold/watch.

Red Flags

  • The PE series is interpolated between quarter-end anchors from a vendor series, not reconciled tick by tick. That is Tier 3; confidence is capped at Medium. The decomposition is arithmetic, not a second valuation model.
  • The denominator is peak-cycle earnings. TTM EPS of $9.31 is up 143% year on year; a low PE on peak AI earnings is a warning, not a bargain.
  • Cash conversion is weak: Q3 CFO/NI is 0.74 and receivables are up 143% year on year. A band on earnings that do not convert to cash is fragile.
  • The band is raw PE, not rate-adjusted; a 2.88% real yield compresses the distribution. P20/P80 is a 60% central band, not a 68% band, and realized coverage is not computed.

Decision Impact

  • Valuation layer: supports undemanding as a cross-check; cannot produce priced for perfection or not solvable alone.
  • Company layer: 14/18 quality, with earnings quality at watch (three screen breaches) and an unresolved governance event.
  • Label: hold/watch. The band alone cannot support add-candidate watch or trim-review.
  • Confidence: Medium. The path is interpolated, the denominator is at a peak, and the 750-observation window is shorter than a full cycle.
  • Output: the figure is a context exhibit, not a target price or a probability of reversion.
