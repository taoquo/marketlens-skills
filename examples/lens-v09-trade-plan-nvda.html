Red Flags

  • The valuation no longer blocks; the evidence does. A lower multiple is not edge without a cash-conversion read.
  • Q3 CFO/NI is 0.74, DSO is 64 days, and receivables are up 143% year on year. The earnings base is growing faster than the cash.
  • The 31% rally on the CEO reinstatement is event-driven, and the next earnings event carries a ±22.2% implied move. Entry timing is poor.
  • Q3 buybacks of $22.1B imply roughly $220 per share, near the 52-week high; the stock is now $155. Capital allocation is adequate, not disciplined.
  • The regime is fragile: HY OAS 352bp, real 10y 2.88%, VIX 35.9. High-beta AI is not aligned with the regime.

Decision Impact

  • Research quality and Deep liquidity cannot pull the label back to tradable. Two overrides hold it at monitor closely: regime and evidence.
  • The delivery-dependence discount cuts position intent one notch. It does not change the label.
  • The upgrade path is now evidence-based, not price-based: cash conversion, governance clearance, and regime stabilization.
  • A louder narrative without stronger evidence is not an upgrade. If the user holds a real position with high AI exposure, this becomes a portfolio-concentration question.
