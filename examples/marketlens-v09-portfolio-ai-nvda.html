Red Flags

  • Three shared assumptions can fail together: real rates up, cloud capex cut, and accounting conventions exposed in a slowdown.
  • A fourth is that the same management teams convert this year's spend into returns above cost of capital; it does not fail ticker by ticker.
  • Gross exposure is not diversification, upstream/downstream is not a hedge, and historical ROIIC is not evidence for this cycle.
  • If two or more holdings breach the same earnings-quality screen, treat it as one accounting practice, not two independent company problems.
